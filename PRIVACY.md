# Privacy Policy — Qiyama

**Last updated:** September 6, 2026

Qiyama (“the app”) is a Tahajjud wake-training app for iPhone. This policy describes what the app does with information on your device.

## Summary

- No account. No sign-in.
- Data stays on your iPhone.
- We do not sell your data.
- We do not track you across apps or websites.
- We do not run advertising analytics in the app.

## Information the app uses

### Location (for Fajr time)

You choose a city (or place) so the app can calculate Fajr and your wake time. That choice is stored on your device. The app does not continuously track your GPS position for advertising or analytics.

### Camera

The camera is used only when you scan your printed mark to prove you left bed (or when you adopt / test a mark). The app does not upload your photos to a server.

### Alarms and notifications

With your permission, the app schedules system alarms and notifications so it can wake you before Fajr — including when the phone is locked or silent. Those permissions are used only for wake training.

### Local app data

Wake times, morning outcomes (out of bed / missed), settings, and your mark token are stored locally on your device (for example in app storage / preferences). This data is not sent to a Qiyama backend, because the app does not operate one.

## Third parties

The app uses on-device system frameworks (such as AlarmKit, User Notifications, MapKit search for city lookup, and the camera). City search may contact Apple’s map services to resolve a place name you type. See Apple’s privacy information for those system services.

## Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. Do not use it if you are under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and this file in the public repository.
